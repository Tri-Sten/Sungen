Our services

End-to-end support

From feasibility study to commissioning, Sungen provides independent engineering expertise at every phase of your photovoltaic project.

Study & Design

Feasibility studies, solar potential analysis, detailed technical design and optimal sizing of photovoltaic plants to maximise energy yield.

Sizing & Modelling

Energy modelling (Archelios PRO), sizing of PV equipment, inverters and battery storage systems. Self-consumption ratio and LCOE optimisation.

Tender Assistance

Specification drafting, EPC bid analysis, supplier and installer selection assistance. Contractual terms negotiation.

Negotiation & Contracts

Technical support during negotiations with EPCs, equipment suppliers and grid operators. Technical contract review and validation.

Owner's Representative (AMO)

Site monitoring, installation quality control, stakeholder coordination, works acceptance and commissioning. Regular client reporting.

Storage & Battery

Expertise in BESS (Battery Energy Storage Systems) sizing. Storage strategy, cost-benefit analysis and PV plant integration.
